Takedawn @VERSION@ for macOS (Apple silicon)

1. Drag Takedawn onto the Applications folder.

2. The first time you open it, macOS stops it: Takedawn is not signed with
   an Apple developer certificate and is not notarised. To allow it:

     - open Takedawn once (macOS says it cannot be opened), choose Done;
     - open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to
       "Takedawn was blocked", choose Open Anyway and confirm.

   On macOS 14 and earlier: right-click (or Control-click) Takedawn in
   Applications, choose Open, then Open again.

   Or, in Terminal:   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/@APP_TITLE@.app"

   You do this once.

3. Takedawn needs the game's data from your own disc: a disc image
   (.iso / .xiso) or an extracted disc folder of Burnout 3: Takedown,
   @DISC@. It checks every file and copies them to
   ~/Library/Application Support/Takedawn/Game. Without the setup window,
   from Terminal:

     "/Applications/@APP_TITLE@.app/Contents/MacOS/Takedawn" --install <image-or-folder>

Saves and settings are in ~/Library/Application Support/Takedawn, the shader
cache in ~/Library/Caches/Takedawn.

How to update
  1. Quit Takedawn.
  2. Open the new disk image and drag Takedawn onto the Applications folder;
     when Finder asks, choose Replace.
  3. The new copy is not signed either, so macOS blocks it once more: repeat
     step 2 above (Open Anyway in Privacy & Security, or the xattr command).
  The saves, settings, game data and shader cache are in the folders above,
  not in the app, and stay as they are; the data is not asked for again.
  Takedawn says when a newer release exists: Settings > Advanced > Check for
  updates (only when you press it; nothing is downloaded).

The licences of everything inside are in
@APP_TITLE@.app/Contents/Resources/licenses.
