Takedawn is a PC port of Burnout 3. This is a community-run lobby for its online play: it lists games and introduces players, the races run between the players' own computers. Want your own server? It is one small program: how to run one. These numbers are counts only; no names and no addresses are kept. An unofficial fan project, not affiliated with or endorsed by EA.
